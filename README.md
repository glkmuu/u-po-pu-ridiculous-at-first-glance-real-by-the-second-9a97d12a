Our entry is a single sentence pitching a startup that sells sealed jars of air from a customer's hometown to homesick expats. It sounds like a joke until the economics land: nostalgia is a real market, and a thirty-dollar breath of home is far cheaper than a nine-hundred-dollar flight, so the absurd product has obvious buyers and an obvious reason to pay. The program writes that one sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> We sell sealed jars of air from your hometown to homesick expats, because nostalgia is a market and a $30 breath of home beats a $900 flight.

141 characters, single line, no trailing newline, UTF-8.
